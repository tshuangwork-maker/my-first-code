Project Detail

Adidas Store Expansion Strategy

A data-driven framework for deciding where Adidas should expand offline stores while balancing profitability and brand reach.

Project Overview

This project builds a strategic evaluation model for offline expansion. Rather than using sales alone, it combines profitability, channel mix, and product structure to identify high-value store opportunities.

Business Question

Which cities and market contexts should Adidas prioritize for store expansion to improve long-term profitability and brand coverage?

Method

  • Store expansion decision framework using multi-factor scoring.
  • White space analysis to locate underserved, high-potential areas.
  • Channel analysis across in-store, online, and outlet performance.
  • Product category analysis for category-market fit by location.
  • Profitability and brand coverage mapping for expansion trade-offs.

Key Insights

  • Top-line sales can hide weak margin quality in some markets.
  • White space regions with strong brand demand remain undercovered.
  • Offline stores create greater lift where channel overlap is complementary.
  • Category depth differs by city, influencing assortment strategy.

Business Implications

Recommendation: prioritize offline expansion in markets with healthy margin pools, white space potential, and category-channel alignment. Use targeted flagship or experience-led formats to strengthen brand visibility without cannibalizing high-performing online traffic.

Limitations

  • Real-estate constraints and lease costs were simplified.
  • Competitor response scenarios were not explicitly simulated.
  • Model assumptions require validation with pilot store rollouts.

Skills Demonstrated

Market strategy, white space analysis, channel diagnostics, profitability analysis, location prioritization, executive recommendation design.
