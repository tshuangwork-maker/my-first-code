Instacart Consumer Behavior Analysis

Business Question: Which customer behaviors indicate repeat purchasing potential?

Method: Cohort analysis, RFM segmentation, and basket trend exploration.

Key Insight: Frequent small-basket shoppers had the strongest 30-day return rates.

Business Implication: Personalized reminders and bundle suggestions can increase retention.

Adidas Store Expansion Strategy

Business Question: Where should Adidas prioritize new store openings?

Method: Market scoring using demographic data, competitor mapping, and sales potential modeling.

Key Insight: Mid-size cities with high sports participation showed high untapped demand.

Business Implication: Expansion into these markets can improve coverage with lower competitive pressure.

Netflix Pricing & Conjoint Analysis

Business Question: What pricing and feature bundles maximize subscription intent?

Method: Conjoint survey analysis and willingness-to-pay estimation.

Key Insight: Ad-free streaming and multi-device access drove the highest utility.

Business Implication: Tiered pricing with clear feature differentiation can reduce churn risk.
