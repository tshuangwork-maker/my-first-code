Projects

Instacart Consumer Behavior Analysis

Business Question: How can grocery platforms identify repeat purchase opportunities and design more targeted retention strategies?

Method: Analyzed ordering time patterns, repeat purchase behavior, basket sequence, association rules, and FRA-based customer segmentation.

Key Insight: Repeat purchase potential was linked to shopping timing, product reorder behavior, and basket position, allowing customers to be grouped into different engagement segments.

Business Implication: Platforms can design reminder campaigns, bundle promotions, and reactivation strategies based on customer lifecycle and basket behavior.

Adidas Store Expansion Strategy

Business Question: Where should Adidas prioritize offline store expansion to improve profitability and brand coverage?

Method: Built a city and channel analysis using sales, profit, product category, and store performance indicators, with a deeper comparison of in-store, online, and outlet channels.

Key Insight: Expansion decisions should not rely only on total sales; channel mix, product category strength, and profitability reveal different market opportunities.

Business Implication: Adidas can prioritize markets where offline stores strengthen premium positioning and complement existing online or outlet performance.

Netflix Pricing & Conjoint Analysis

Business Question: How should Netflix design and price an ad-supported subscription plan?

Method: Built a utility matrix, simulated market share, estimated demand, and forecasted profit under different pricing and advertising load scenarios.

Key Insight: Profitability depends not only on subscription price, but also on advertising revenue, user preference trade-offs, and feature bundle design.

Business Implication: A tiered pricing strategy can balance user acquisition, retention, and monetization while reducing the risk of cannibalizing existing plans.
