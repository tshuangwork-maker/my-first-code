Project Detail

Instacart Consumer Behavior Analysis

This project explores how customer ordering rhythms and basket patterns can improve retention strategy for online grocery platforms.

Project Overview

I analyzed transactional behavior data to identify repeat purchase signals and customer lifecycle differences. The goal was to convert behavioral patterns into clear retention levers for CRM and campaign planning.

Business Question

How can grocery platforms identify high-potential repeat buyers and personalize interventions to increase reorder frequency?

Method

  • Order timing analysis by weekday and hour.
  • Reorder rate and basket sequence analysis.
  • Association rule mining for bundle opportunities.
  • FRA segmentation for lifecycle and engagement scoring.

Key Insights

  • Reorder behavior clusters around predictable shopping windows.
  • Basket position influences the probability of repeat purchase.
  • Segment-level differences require different retention tactics.

Business Implications

Teams can design reminder nudges, bundle promotions, and reactivation campaigns based on timing and segment behavior rather than relying on one-size-fits-all messaging.

Limitations

  • Promotion exposure and price elasticity were not fully modeled.
  • Analysis is limited by observed behavior, not stated preference.
  • External factors such as seasonality may shift reorder patterns.

Skills Demonstrated

SQL, R, customer segmentation, association analysis, retention strategy.
