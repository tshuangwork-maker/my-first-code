Project Detail

Netflix Pricing & Conjoint Analysis

A conjoint-based pricing study to evaluate ad-supported plan design and monetization trade-offs.

Project Overview

This project tested subscription package preferences and quantified the revenue implications of different price and ad-load combinations for a new Netflix tier.

Business Question

How should Netflix structure and price an ad-supported plan to maximize profit while minimizing cannibalization risk?

Method

  • Built utility models from conjoint survey responses.
  • Simulated market share under multiple feature-price bundles.
  • Estimated demand and contribution margin by scenario.
  • Compared outcomes with sensitivity analysis on ad revenue levels.

Key Insights

  • Price elasticity varies by ad tolerance and feature expectations.
  • Moderate ad load can unlock incremental profit at scale.
  • Bundle design influences switching behavior across existing tiers.

Business Implications

A tiered approach with clear value separation can support acquisition, retention, and monetization simultaneously, especially when ad load is optimized to protect user experience.

Limitations

  • Stated preference data may differ from real market behavior.
  • Long-term churn effects were not fully observed.
  • Advertising demand fluctuations can affect realized profitability.

Skills Demonstrated

Conjoint analysis, pricing strategy, demand simulation, scenario modeling, and business model recommendation.
